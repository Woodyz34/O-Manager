# Sounds in "Run it yourself (3D)"

The footsteps, landings, clothing rustle and voices (groans, grunts) in `Assets/Resources/RunSounds` come from:

"Oculus Audio Pack 01" by Oculus VR, LLC (https://developers.meta.com/vr/downloads/package/oculus-audio-pack-1/),
licensed under CC BY 4.0. Files renamed; OGG previews transcoded from the original WAVs.
Taken from github.com/facebookincubator/Meta-open-sound (corpora/oculus-audio-pack).

The credit is shown in the game in the "3D settings" window (RunSounds.Credit).
The breathing and the beeps are made in code. Recorded breaths can be added as breath_man_1 .. breath_man_10 and
breath_woman_1 .. breath_woman_10 (one whole breath, in and out, per file).
